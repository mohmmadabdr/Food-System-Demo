## Brand & Style

This design system embodies modern culinary craftsmanship fused with enterprise-grade operational efficiency. Built for high-volume multi-brand restaurant groups, boutique hospitality concepts, and ghost kitchens, the aesthetic pairs the rich, tactile warmth of upscale dining with the clarity of a precision SaaS management engine.

### Personality & Tone
- **Gastronomic Sophistication:** Warm, appetizing earth tones replace sterile tech blues, imparting an inviting, sensory atmosphere that honors fine ingredients and culinary artistry.
- **Enterprise Precision:** Clean line ratios, disciplined spatial geometry, and crisp typographic hierarchies ensure operators can process orders, analyze margins, and route delivery runs with split-second clarity.
- **Bilingual Fluency:** Conceived from the outset for seamless bidirectional rendering (LTR and RTL), with native parity between Latin and Arabic typographic metrics.

### Visual Style
- **Warm Minimalist Tactility:** Clean surfaces punctuated by deeply saturated terracotta and espresso elements, reinforced with subtle warm-amber glow highlights and micro-borders.
- **Multi-Brand Chameleon (White-Label):** Surfaces remain neutral, crisp, and parchment-clean so individual restaurant brand identities shine through imagery and custom accent overrides without visual dissonance.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from raw culinary elements: terracotta baking stones, rich pulled espresso, unrefined brass fittings, and warm parchment paper.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#C25E3E` — Terracotta):** Signals primary actions, order statuses in progress, live checkout buttons, and key interactive highlights.
- **Secondary (`#1C1917` — Deep Espresso):** Anchors high-priority typography, navigation bars, modal headers, and dense tabular data. Provides sharp contrast against light backdrops.
- **Tertiary (`#D99B26` — Saffron Gold):** Reserved for VIP tier badges, revenue velocity indicators, critical prep alerts, and culinary rating stars.
- **Neutral Base (`#FBF9F6` — Warm Crisp Parchment):** The main canvas surface. Eliminates eye fatigue from stark `#FFFFFF` glare during extended kitchen and POS shifts while remaining clean and modern.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** High-level card backgrounds, elevated sheets, and popovers.
- **Surface Borders (`#E7E1D8`):** Organic, low-contrast borders providing subtle structure without heavy visual weight.

### Semantic Status
- **Success (`#2D6A4F` / Olive Forest):** Completed tickets, settled payments, active store statuses.
- **Warning (`#D97706` / Burnt Amber):** Pending approvals, delay thresholds reached, 86'd inventory alerts.
- **Destructive (`#B91C1C` / Pomegranate):** Canceled orders, refund actions, offline POS terminals.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes balanced optical weights and exceptional legibility across dense ordering boards and clean customer menus.

### Font Pairing & Arabic (RTL) Strategy
- **Primary Typeface:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides contemporary geometric stability with friendly, humanist curves suitable for modern hospitality.
- **Arabic Localization Pairing:** When rendering in Arabic (`dir="rtl"`), the system mandates fallback to **Cairo** or **Tajawal** (e.g., `font-family: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', 'Cairo', 'Tajawal', sans-serif`).
  - **Cairo** is mapped directly to `display`, `headline-*`, and `label-*` styles to preserve vertical rhythm and robust geometric structure.
  - **Tajawal** is mapped to `body-*` levels for its fluid, comfortable reading experience across long Arabic ingredient lists and notes.
- **RTL Metric Adjustments:** In Arabic mode, increase line heights by `+2px` across all body tiers to accommodate Arabic ascenders and descenders without clipping. Eliminate negative tracking (`letter-spacing`) on all Arabic text to preserve natural cursive glyph connections.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt baseline grid configured for high-density SaaS management and fluid responsive ordering.

### Grid & Viewport Adaptation
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout. Employs a collapsible persistent master navigation drawer (260px expanded, 72px icon-only rail). The live order tracking board operates on a multi-column Kanban format with fixed card widths and horizontal scroll overflow when exceeding 4 live stations. Outer margin defaults to `margin-lg` (3rem).
- **Tablet / Kitchen Display System (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Navigation shifts to a persistent top bar or compact icon rail. Modals scale to sheet drawers sliding from the contextual edge.
- **Mobile Handheld (<= 767px):** 4-column layout with `margin-sm` (1rem). Navigation moves to an anchored bottom tab bar with dynamic floating action trays for quick checkout.

### Bidirectional Layout Rules
- All layouts utilize logical CSS properties (`padding-inline`, `margin-inline`, `inset-inline-start`, `text-align: start`).
- In RTL mode, kanban pipeline progressions, timeline steps, and breadcrumbs mirror horizontally from right to left.
- Numeric currency symbols anchor contextually: ISO codes (e.g., `SAR`, `AED`) follow the standard flow of the active language, while numeric values retain LTR numeral sequences.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through warm, ambient shadows and layered surface tints rather than cool gray drop-shadows. This preserves the inviting atmosphere of the culinary canvas.

### Spatial Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#FBF9F6`. Deep baseline canvas representing inactive screen real estate.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Containers):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-fine border `1px solid #E7E1D8`. Shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered States, Menus, Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF`. Shadow: `0px 4px 16px rgba(194, 94, 62, 0.08), 0px 8px 24px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.06)`. Note the warm terracotta ambient bleed.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-over Drawers, POS Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` with backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) over an espresso veil (`rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.4)`). Shadow: `0px 16px 40px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a tactile, organic curvature that softens technical data without looking childish. 

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Base (0.5rem / 8px):** Input fields, inline tags, small buttons, table row selections, kitchen ticket badges.
- **Large (1rem / 16px):** Menu item cards, operational metrics widgets, live kitchen kanban columns, master modal containers.
- **Extra Large (1.5rem / 24px):** Customer hero banners, multi-brand brand switchers, and elevated marketing callouts.
- **Full / Pill:** Status indicator badges, filter pill groups, floating quick-order counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Terracotta (`#C25E3E`), text in `#FFFFFF`, with a subtle hover transition to `#A94F32`. Active state compresses slightly (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF` with a `1px solid #E7E1D8` border, text in Espresso (`#1C1917`). On hover, transitions to `#FBF9F6` with border tint `#C25E3E`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, Espresso text, with an organic terracotta hover fill at 8% opacity.
- **Icon Support:** All trailing/leading icon placements adapt automatically based on `dir="rtl"` flags.

### Cards
- **Menu Item Card:** Constructed with a white background (`#FFFFFF`), rounded-large corners (`16px`), and a delicate `1px` border. Images use an aspect ratio of `4:3` with subtle zoom on card hover. Price appears in bold Espresso, with modifiers highlighted in muted body text.
- **Order Ticket Card:** Color-coded top edge accent (3px) displaying ticket age. Incorporates high-legibility bold monospace order IDs, item counts with quantity badges, and quick-action state toggles.

### Badge Indicators & Status Chips
- **Structure:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with `padding: 4px 10px`, font size `label-sm`.
- **Live Prep:** Terracotta surface at 10% opacity with solid Terracotta text and an animated pulsing status dot.
- **Ready for Pickup:** Forest green at 10% opacity with solid Forest text.
- **VIP / High Value:** Saffron gold (`#D99B26`) background at 12% opacity with dark gold text.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height of 44px for touch and cursor accessibility. Border `1px solid #E7E1D8`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active focus state triggers a crisp `2px` ring in Terracotta (`#C25E3E`) with zero offset. Labels sit above in `label-md` Espresso.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px size with `rounded-sm` (4px) for checkboxes and full circles for radios. Checked state fills with Terracotta and an off-white indicator mark.

### Lists & Tables
- **Kitchen / Operational Tables:** Striped background alternation utilizing `#FFFFFF` and `#FBF9F6`. Row height minimum 56px to support touch interaction on tablet terminals. Text in start-alignment, monetary values tabular-aligned.

### Multi-Brand Switcher (Domain-Specific)
- A segmented pill or dropdown selector housing brand logos alongside operational status rings (open/closed). Active brand applies an Espresso badge outline with subtle Terracotta accenting.
